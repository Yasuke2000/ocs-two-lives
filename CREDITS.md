  Fuse, illustrated by Mitz Vah; published by Micro Magazine / Kodansha. All rights to the original work belong to
  them.
- **Tensura: Reincarnated** (the `tensura` mod) — by the ManasMods team. Some builds in this repo use pieces of the
  mod's own structures, kept as reference in `blueprints/assets/` and built into our structures with credit:
  - goblin village pieces (`data/tensura/structure/goblin_village/…`) in early Tempest (`blueprints/tempest.py`);
  - King Gazel Dwargo's throne room (the mod's boss arena, exported from our own world) in Dwargon
    (`blueprints/dwargon.py`).
  If the authors would rather we didn't include these files, we will remove them and place the pieces from the
  installed mod at runtime instead.
- **Great Sage** addon, **ManasCore**, and every other mod in `pack/mods/` — by their respective authors, under
  their own licences; the pack only links to them (packwiz), it does not redistribute their jars.
- Map references: fan-made Tensura world maps (political and illustrated); our grid in `pack/config/ttl/` is our own
  trace, the reference images are not in the repo.
- **Demon Lord seat builds** (`blueprints/clayman_castle.py`, `forgotten_dragon_city.py`, `laura.py`) are our own code. For massing and palette we looked at structure files of other mods in the pack as visual references only and copied nothing: Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks (Citadel; "All Rights Reserved") and Towns and Towers (jungle, savanna and temple villages; CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0, no derivatives), plus Tensura's village pieces for the thatch and mud style.
