# The Sealed Cave (Veldora's prison, Rimuru's birthplace)

Build: `blueprints/sealed_cave.py` (88 x 50 x 88, anchor (44, 5, 82) on the path outside the cave mouth), placed by
`design/builds/sealed_cave.toml` on the canon landmark "Sealed Cave (Veldora's prison, Rimuru's birthplace)"
(ref [1745, 1078] -> world about (-256, -343) at the 14,000-block continent width; Jura forest, north-west of Tempest,
land in `canon_map.txt`).

## Canon (what the sources say)

- The cave is in the Great Jura Forest. Veldora Tempest was sealed in it about 300 years before the main story by a
  masked hero using the seal skill "Infinity Prison" (the English wiki's name; the owner and some translations say
  "Unlimited Imprisonment"). One search extract credited Chronoa instead: unreliable, not used.
- Rimuru wakes in the same cave after his death and meets Veldora there; the two take the name Tempest.
- Veldora's aura filled the cave with magicules, which is why magic ore and hipokute grass grow there. Later it became
  Tempest's hipokute farm under Gabiru's supervision (and a dungeon in the wiki's terms).
- In our Life 2 timeline Rimuru has already swallowed Veldora and left, so the seal is spent and the dragon is gone.

## What we built (and which parts are our interpretation)

Canon gives the place, the seal and the magicules; it does not give a floor plan. So, **our interpretation**:

- A rocky, mossy hill (about 70 blocks across, 27 high) in a forest clearing with the mouth in its south face; weathered
  stones flank the mouth.
- A winding mouth passage, then the antechamber: a cave pond, magic ore (`tensura:magic_ore`) in the walls, glow-berry
  vines, dripstone, and a small hipokute plot (`tensura:hipokute_grass`, age 3, on watered farmland) for the later farm.
- A narrow passage, then the sealed chamber: a round dome (28 x 26 across, 15 high) of deepslate with amethyst and
  `tensura:deepslate_magic_ore` in the walls; in the floor a double ring and a hexagram of teal copper and sea lanterns
  (the runes of the seal, dim and cracked); a black dais where the dragon lay with snapped chains, claw gouges scoring the
  floor, six standing stones (two toppled) and an armillary of three light-blue glass rings, broken into arcs. A small
  basin by the wall marks where a slime woke up.
- Lore link to the Tempest zone: the magicule that is deadly around world (0, 0) comes from this cave in canon. No game
  logic is attached to that.
