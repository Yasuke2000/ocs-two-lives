# Laura: lore behind blueprints/laura.py

The build reference for Laura, the new capital of the Beast Kingdom of Eurazania ruled by the Beast King Carrion.
Geography and placement are in [world.md](world.md) §B, the placement contract in `design/builds/laura.toml`. Read this
before changing the blueprint.

The geography entry describes Eurazania’s agriculture, the loss of its capital to Milim and Rimuru’s offer of reconstruction aid. Carrion’s official anime portrait supplies the reference for the gold-and-brown palette. The lion gable, stilt houses and market plan are our interpretation of the rebuilt city. [Japanese Wikipedia, Eurazania](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/転生したらスライムだった件#ユーラザニア) · [Official character art, Carrion](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/carrion)

| A nation of beastmen, not a refined court *(our interpretation)* | A timber longhouse-style hall with a tall thatch roof, stilt houses with ladders | — |

## Layout in short (blueprint coordinates, south = +z)

Gate in the south wall (x 52–60, banners, two round towers) → stone avenue → the market plaza (fountain, stalls) → the
palace terrace (z 4–48, grand steps at x 50–62) → the great hall (x 44–68, z 10–44) with the lion throne at its north end.
Stilt houses on both sides, orchards in the four corners, a pool behind the hall.

## Reference builds looked at

Tensura's goblin village `palm` set and its lizardman village pieces (thatch and timber style); Towns and Towers'
`jungle_tribal`, `sparse_jungle_polynesian` and `swamp_boat` villages for stilt-house proportions, as visual references
only (CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0, nothing copied). Create's structure files are only test contraptions and were not useful.
