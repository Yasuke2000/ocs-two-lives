# The Abandoned Adit

A broken length of rail crosses the yard and runs into a narrow cut between the hillsides. Miners once wound a haul line around the timber drum beside the mouth. Four oak prop frames still carry the roof. Follow the track to the rockfall, or turn east between the frames to find a lit tool recess and workbench. Short stacks of pit timber and a heap of waste rock mark the last workings outside. The mine, its abandonment and its builders are our interpretation of ordinary Mortal Realm industry.

The southeast workings include a timber sorting area with props stacked on crosswise sleepers and a covered cutting bench. A rail siding turns north from the yard into a stepped open quarry. A drainage channel beside it collects water. Walk from local (23, 4, 35) to the quarry floor near (52, 4, 24) or the stonecutters’ shed at (46, 4, 48). Rails run alongside the foot route. Two template slabs meet at z 35.

The template measures 73 × 24 × 68. Its south-road arrival at local (23, 4, 35) corresponds to a survey centre of (−1040, 240) in the Mortal Realm. The survey can move the site up to 64 blocks and fits the base with an eight-block margin. The walk stays at local y 4 inside the adit. The collapse deliberately closes the end. Loot chests, spawners and exposed ore veins are absent. The underground passage is part of this structure.
