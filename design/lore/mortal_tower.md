# Hermit’s Tower

The hermit keeps a few flowers behind a low stone edging. A winding gravel track climbs six contours past pollarded oaks. At the turn, a side track serves three rows of medicinal flowers on the hillside. The summit path leads to a narrow south doorway and a workroom. A ladder against the north wall passes the sleeping floor and reaches a glazed reading room above the stone shell. A chair, lectern and low bookcase fit beneath the hipped deepslate roof. The lantern over the reading desk is visible through the upper windows.

The template measures 71 × 34 × 73 blocks. The garden approach is surveyed from (−300, 1030) in the Mortal Realm. Both floor openings align with the ladder. The slope around the garden is fitted by the survey placer, and plants outside the template remain seed-dependent.

**Our interpretation.** The free-standing, compact tower houses of the English and Scottish borders inspired the stone lower storeys. The hermit has adapted the upper floor for reading and sleeping below it. The herb plot, timber window band and dark tiled roof tell that domestic story.
