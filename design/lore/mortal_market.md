# The Folk Market

A bell hangs over the northern end of the square between a brick-roofed house and a spruce-roofed one. Their doors face the public reading arcade. The east–west lane crosses the south approach between two striped canopies. The cloth stall has a loom, the adjoining yard a composter, the opposite counter a grindstone, and the arcade a lectern. Both homes have a bed and table. The desks, shops and residents' daily routines are our interpretation of village life in the Mortal Realm.

The east–west road continues to a covered weigh house. Its timber beam suspends two weighing pans beneath a tiled roof. The south lane branches between a small orchard and an open cattle pen with a hitching rail and shade tree. Walk through the pen entrance at local (47, 4, 50) or follow the main road to its south boundary at z 68. Two template slabs meet at z 35.

The 73 × 25 × 69 template places its arrival at local (24, 4, 36), south of both stalls. Its survey starts at world (180, −780) in the Mortal Realm, searches up to 64 blocks and has an eight-block terrain margin. Villagers can claim the beds and workstations according to vanilla rules. The template itself supplies the buildings and furnishings. It does not spawn villagers, seed trades or award the Folk chapter's crafting and trading objectives. The actual population and road joins need a server check.

The four workstations come from the pack's **Folk of the Realm** chapter. We checked the vanilla 1.21.1 village meeting-point asset `minecraft:village/plains/town_centers/plains_meeting_point_3`, Blumund's Life 2 market square and the existing Mortal spawn's merchant row for scale and use. Houses use the pack's shared house helper, both stalls and the bell arcade are original blockwork, and the palette is entirely vanilla. No external structure was copied.
