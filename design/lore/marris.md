# Marris: lore behind blueprints/marris.py

The build reference for Marris, capital of the Kingdom of Falmuth (later Farmenas), with the royal castle and a
Western Holy Church presence. Geography and placement are in [world.md](world.md) §F. Read this before changing the
blueprint.

**Research note (18/11/2026).** tensura.fandom.com returns HTTP 402 to direct fetches. Facts come from Wikipedia's
character list and search extracts. Canon text gives no description of the capital's buildings, and the Wikipedia
list has **no entry for Marris**: the name comes from world.md (the fan map) and is *unverified*. Everything about
how the city looks is *our interpretation*.

## Our interpretation (canon is silent)

- **Look.** Pale stone and white quartz, verdigris copper roofs (the preview shows them orange), slate roofs on the
  houses, blue and white heraldry, gold trim on the church. Taller and richer than Blumund's timber town.
- **Western Holy Church.** A cathedral in white quartz: nave with buttresses, blue stained glass, a rose window over
  the doors, pews, an aisle, an altar with candles at the west end, a bell tower with a copper spire. The Church's real
  symbol is not used. (The Church's seat is Lubelius, see world.md §L.)
- **Size.** 136 x 112 blocks; gate towers 17 high, palace 18 high with four corner towers to 30, bell tower 30.

## Layout in short

Royal road -> great gate (z 92-96) -> King's Avenue (townhouses) -> Royal Plaza (x 24-112, z 38-76, fountain monument
at 68,57, banner poles) with the cathedral on the west and the customs house on the east -> castle gatehouse
(z 34-36) -> courtyard -> palace (x 40-96, z 8-21) with the throne hall, a gallery floor and copper hip roofs.
