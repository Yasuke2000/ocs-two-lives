# Nasca — Imperial audience court

From the southern approach, two standards mark the way onto a parade ground. Pale lines in the paving mark files for the guard. Their two galleries face one another across the court. A flight of five broad steps leads to the open audience pavilion, where three chairs stand before a red screen. From the seats, a visitor can see through the columns all the way to the standards. A raised amber-glass clerestory lights the centre of the hall. Copper scrolls crown its dark green roof.

## Canon and interpretation

[Official World Guide A-21](https://www.ten-sura.com/map#A-21) supplies the broad eastern polity; [author WN136 L157](https://ncode.syosetu.com/n6316bn/140/#L157) names its capital. This is qualified WN evidence, not independent confirmation of a LN/anime city plan. [R76 access review](../../world/nasca-source-topology.md) distinguishes later four-sided access and wooded-road evidence. The fan political trace does not verify a mountain bearing; C05 remains open. [R93 source/era review](../../world/empire-chronology-r93.md).

The current own-world reservation is **(3200, -1800), radius90**, with exported ground75. Its numerical placement is our interpretation. No survey of the complete capital is supplied by the audience-court model.

The audience court, gallery plan, parade markings, red flame devices, furniture and roof colours are **our interpretation**. The consulted sources supplied no plan or verified view of this building. The three chairs provide space for an audience without implying a particular scene, doctrine or fixed trio of canon characters.

## Existing court design and historical placement

The template measures **85 × 42 × 79 blocks** (x, y, z). The following Lerfing placement is historical and does not govern the own-world map. The southern approach at local **(42, 5, 66)** is the survey anchor for **(5100, −2680)** in region E, in the valley north of the volcano on Lerfing's map (plains, relief 1). That historical box was assigned to E land. The survey may move up to 96 blocks and accepts at most 10% wet samples. The three z slabs begin at 0, 27 and 54. The road becomes wheel tracks at the south boundary. Check the skirt at the road and the actual sightline from the surveyed approach on the server.
