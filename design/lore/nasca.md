# Nasca — Imperial audience court

From the southern approach, two standards mark the way onto a parade ground. Pale lines in the paving mark files for the guard. Their two galleries face one another across the court. A flight of five broad steps leads to the open audience pavilion, where three chairs stand before a red screen. From the seats, a visitor can see through the columns all the way to the standards. A raised amber-glass clerestory lights the centre of the hall. Copper scrolls crown its dark green roof.

## Canon and interpretation

The official world map identifies the Eastern Empire as the vast polity in the east and gives its full name, including Nasca. The novel's imperial government concentrates both political and military power in Rudra. The Imperial Guardians protect the emperor. These relationships suggested a court that serves both an audience and a muster. The original map trace supplies the location of the capital and a separate point for Velgrynd's mountain northwest of it. [Official map, Eastern Empire](https://www.ten-sura.com/map/#A-21) · [Novel setting and references, Rudra](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/転生したらスライムだった件#ルドラ) · [Pack political-map audit, point 37](../../docs/audits/world-audit-2026-11.md) · [Political reference plate](https://i.redd.it/q1p4ksudqyah1.png)

The audience court, gallery plan, parade markings, red flame devices, furniture and roof colours are **our interpretation**. The consulted sources supplied no plan or verified view of this building. The three chairs provide space for an audience without implying a particular scene, doctrine or fixed trio of canon characters.

## Building and placement

The template measures **85 × 42 × 79 blocks** (x, y, z). The southern approach at local **(42, 5, 66)** is the survey anchor for **(5100, −2680)** in region E, in the valley north of the volcano on Lerfing's map (plains, relief 1). The initial box lies entirely on E land. The survey may move up to 96 blocks and accepts at most 10% wet samples. The three z slabs begin at 0, 27 and 54. The road becomes wheel tracks at the south boundary. Check the skirt at the road and the actual sightline from the surveyed approach on the server. [Blueprint](../../blueprints/nasca.py) · [Placement manifest](../builds/nasca.toml)

The shared town helpers supply ground, stairs, lamps and the lighting test. The two pylons, parade pavement, pavilion and galleries are original geometry. We checked the existing Gazel throne asset, Tensura's underground lizardman throne room and Iron's Spells' citadel entrance tower. Their different settings and footprints called for a new plan. No third-party structure, asset or heraldic design is embedded. Tensura and Iron's Spells list All Rights Reserved. The pinned mod structure inventory contained no Nasca-labelled template.
