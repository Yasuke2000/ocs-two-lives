# Character skins

Each skin is drawn for a specific arm model. Classic uses four pixels across the front of each arm and slim uses three. The 28 rows cover two Free Guild roles and 26 other story subjects, including six Walpurgis statues. Use the anime sheets for the costume and the episode galleries for details visible in a particular scene. The linked Japanese wiki entries identify the characters, including their aliases, species and changes of form. The guild desk clerk is the pack's original character.

## Pack costume choices

Fuze keeps the official face shape and greying temples. For the Blumund contract desk, his skin design calls for stubble, a scar and practical leather guild gear. Kaijin wears a smith's leather apron and a brown beard. Kaijin uses a full 64 × 64 classic skin and a uniform 0.8 runtime scale, measured from his feet. The full-size atlas preserves the detail on his face and apron.

The Kijin use distinct dress silhouettes. Benimaru wears a dark kimono with red accents. Shuna keeps her white and red shrine attire, Shion her dark tailored suit, Souei his close-fitting ninja layers and Hakurou his white hakama. Treyni's pale-green dress carries a leaf motif. Milim's version pairs her pink twin tails with black and violet gothic clothing. The table records the visual reference for each adaptation.

Garm, Dold and Mildo are three brothers who settle in Tempest with their fellow craftsman Kaijin. Garm, the eldest, makes armour. Dold specialises in jewellery. Mildo, the youngest, lays out buildings and streets. Kaijin is a smith whose own younger brother is Kaido of the Dwargon guard. [Tempest lore](tempest.md) · [English character list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_That_Time_I_Got_Reincarnated_as_a_Slime_characters)

Louis serves Luminous Valentine as Holy Emperor of Lubelius. Canon identifies Louis as a vampire, Roy as his younger twin who stood in for Luminous as Demon Lord, and Luminous as the sovereign they served. The pack presents Louis as the human-facing church patron in white and gold. The NPC ID refers to Louis himself. [Louis](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/louis) · [Roy](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/roy) · [Luminous](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/luminus)

The six effigies are the pack's interpretation of Walpurgis. The episode galleries show Frey’s jewel, Guy’s cheek markings, Leon’s collar and Ramiris’s bodice. Carrion’s and Dagruel’s details follow their official full-body character sheets. The published banquet preview frames checked offered no identifiable view of either. Their costumes establish rank and identity. Pale limestone highlights mark raised edges of wings, jewellery and fabric, while deeper grey defines the cuts between them. All six use the classic arm model. Frey’s feathered wings and Ramiris’s four fairy wings fold across the back and sleeves so they read on the standard player mesh.

The publisher’s chapter 7 group portrait gives the dwarves’ silhouettes, including the bald crown and knotted scarf. Its caption does not individually label them. The official glossary credits Garm with Kabal’s armour. We used character listings to check the English name variants Dord and Myrd. The Slime Diaries winter episode mentions the dwarves working with Kaijin. Its published gallery and the other eleven Diaries galleries did not yield an individually labelled trio. The individual workwear colours, straps, tools and rear views are our interpretation. ## Easy NPC handoff

On the reviewed main revision (`7a62cc3`), the architecture handoff names `design/architecture/story-characters.md`, but that file is absent. This set follows the 28 IDs in the roster Yasuke supplied. An inspection of the pinned Tensura 2.0.1.5 entity registries and translation keys found no dedicated canon entity for the listed subjects. The IDs label pack art, including the clerk and six statues. Tempest already represents Rigurd and Gobta using named goblins. Check their existing spawn points before introducing story NPCs.

For Easy NPC 7.19.0, have the preset generator write the resource location in the entity’s `SkinData` compound. For example, Fuze’s entity data would include:

```snbt
SkinData: {Type: "RESOURCE_LOCATION", Texture: "ttl:textures/entity/npc/fuze.png"}
```

Choose `easy_npc:humanoid` for classic rows and `easy_npc:humanoid_slim` for slim rows in both the entity’s `id` and the preset metadata’s `entityTypeId`. The PNG carries no arm-width metadata. Keep a valid fallback variant. The current guild generator writes the TOML `skin` value to `VariantType` and hardcodes the classic humanoid, so putting the texture path in that field alone would leave the built-in look in use.

After merge, Claude can wire the two guild roles in `design/data/npcs.toml` and the preset generator, regenerate presets and pack metadata, and bump `settings.rev` so already-spawned guild residents refresh. The other 26 need story-specific placement, including the six statue positions. Living characters also need their role-specific dialog. In-game review should include front and back, moving arms, overlay visibility and a previously spawned guild resident.
