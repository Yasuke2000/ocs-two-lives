# Treyni's shrine (authored dryad altar)

## Current own-world geography and source limits — round57

Current authored reservations are grove(320,470),radius56,and shrine(500,600),radius36. The imported-map coordinates,river placement and +38 offset below are historical implementation context. Neither reservation establishes the separate canonical treant community. [Direct source review](../../docs/research/tensura-world-2026-11-19/treant-route-context-r57.md) verifies guardianship and qualified WN sanctuary/route context,not a surveyed sanctuary border or sovereign half of Jura. ## The old wood round the shrine (19/11/2026)

The owner: Treyni is the dryad guardian of the Forest of Jura, so her shrine belongs deep in old, dense forest; on Lerfing's
map it stood on an open grass slope (tree cover 2 % at r 25..80, 6 % at r 80..180). `life/forests.js` plants the patch
`treyni` once (`design/data/forests/patches.toml`): 69 of Tensura's ancient-forest giants (the same trees as the grove's)
and 1,066 dark oak, fancy oak, birch, spruce and azalea trees at r 25..180 round the shrine; planned canopy 76 %. The
shrine's and the grove tree's recorded boxes (+6 / +4) and an approach lane from the shrine's west gate (the living gate)
towards Tempest, 190 blocks long and 8 wide, stay open; water and Lerfing's keepouts too. Our interpretation.

## Canon

- Treyni is the eldest of three dryad sisters (Treyni, Trya, Doris). Dryads are elementals incarnated in plant life (Dryas
  trees) and guard the Great Jura Forest; Treyni was its manager and guardian under Veldora. Later she and her sisters moved
  to Ramiris's labyrinth and became her subordinates; Rimuru transferred Treyni's spirit into a doll made from the Great Spirit
  Tree.
- The reached sources do not verify an east-of-Ameld sanctuary boundary or a dryad-governed political half of Jura. That was an imported-map/project premise,not established canon. No altar or shrine appearance is established by the selected primary passages.

## What we built (all our interpretation)

A round mossy dais with a ring of green runes, a young azalea tree (a seedling of the ancient tree) four blocks north of the arrival stone, three
seats for the three sisters each with a potted sapling, four log posts with lanterns and vines, and a living gate of two
bent oak logs on the west side. A chain fixes its hanging lantern to the arch. The east-side seats face inward, with the higher side of each stair at the rear. Lit with lanterns, froglight and a shroomlight in the crown.

Treyni’s official character art shows her surrounded by curling greenery. The Japanese official profile establishes her treant guardianship; it does not supply the sister-count attribution previously attached to that link. The sister relationship remains separately sourced through secondary character references. The dais and seedling are original.
