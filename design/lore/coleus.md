# Coleus — the palace of Herbaria

From the oasis, two small turquoise domes frame the start of a straight royal stair. The stair climbs a banded sandstone hill, with a turn onto the two garden terraces halfway up. Under the highest dome a visitor passes through a shaded loggia into the audience chamber. Two facing runs of benches and a blue runner draw the eye to a single seat on a raised dais.

INEI's production painting shows the palace high above the town, a long inclined approach with domed buildings near its foot, narrow pinnacles around the central drum, and water and palms in the foreground. These features determined the silhouette and the direction of travel. The anime credits Kenichiro Tomiyasu of INEI for concept art. Fuse wrote the original booklet story which the three episodes adapt. [Official production painting](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/coleus/introduction) · [Official visual announcement](https://www.ten-sura.com/news/anime/tensura/news/post/8574) · [Staff credits](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/coleus/staffcast)

In *Visions of Coleus*, Yuuki sends Rimuru to investigate the succession dispute between two princes. The royal family gives the architecture a human scale. The east pavilion has a canopied bed, a bookstand and water at hand for Princess Zenobia. The west pavilion holds a table for private talks. Roses flank both doorways in a nod to the final episode, *Purple and Roses*. The room assignments, furnishings, paths and precise layout are **our interpretation**. Cyan tile and weathered copper distinguish the five domes against ochre stone. ## Placement and scale

The structure spans **85 × 47 × 79 blocks** in x, y and z, split into three north–south sections. The anchor at **(42, 4, 72)** is on the walking level at the stair foot. The exact landmark **Coleus** starts the terrain survey at **x −4151, z 370**. That point is dry V land on the pack map. Its 48-block search also has room for the entire footprint on dry land. The unshifted rectangle crosses a mapped watercourse and the V/L border.

Coleus is an independent kingdom. The official anime places it between Ingrassia and Lubelius. The fan cartographer says they deliberately moved it across the river towards Damargania. The build uses the pack's existing point, and the grid letter is its terrain selector. Server terrain will determine the final position, height and fit of the oasis bank. The local blueprints and assets and the 77 pinned mod inventories contained no named Coleus or Herbaria template.
