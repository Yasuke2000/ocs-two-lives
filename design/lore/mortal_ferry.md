# River Ferry and Toll House

The towpath reaches a timber-framed toll house with a sheltered opening for weighing a payment. A lectern holds the toll clerk’s place beside the counter. Outside, four lantern piles frame the landing. The light oak punt has darker raised ends, a chain leading past a hand winch, and a barrel on the stern. Players can walk across the tied-up vessel to the opposite bank.

The template measures 79 × 16 × 77 blocks. Its western towpath anchor is surveyed from (840, −230) in the Mortal Realm. The survey looks for dry land. Between the two barred culverts, a two-block-deep clay-and-gravel cut bends through reed shelves. The towpath follows the western bank past the repair slip and its timber rollers. Both dry roadway ends reach beyond the banks. Both ends terminate inside the template, so the crossing also works on a seed without a river here. The punt stays moored.

**Our interpretation.** At Reedham in Norfolk a chain ferry carries vehicles across the River Yare. The guiding chain and two opposing landings informed this much smaller foot crossing. The clerk, timber gables and toll arrangement belong to the Mortal Realm. [Reedham Ferry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reedham_Ferry)
