# Great Holy Mountain

The empire honours the Scorch Dragon Velgrynd as its guardian. The mountain associated with her is also called Scorching God Mountain. The location entry traces this tradition to Fuse’s light novel, volume 14, chapter 2. The official anime map identifies the Eastern Empire. The pack puts it on the north-west flank of the red crater of Lerfing's Eastern Empire, south of Nasca, at x/z **5120, −1860** (ground y 109), inside region E. [Location and chapter citation](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Scorching_God_Mountain) · [Yen Press, volume 14](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975314477-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-14-light-novel) · [Official anime map](https://www.ten-sura.com/map/#A-21) · [Pack atlas and fan-map projection](../../docs/audits/world-audit-2026-11.md)

**Our interpretation.** From the south, a traveller sees a dark broken rim above the imperial road. The climb ends in a cut-stone arcade where benches face a five-tongued flame behind amber glass. The floor of the summit vent catches the same colour. The rock profile, processional road and votive gallery are original designs. The sources establish the guardian and the mountain; they leave its Minecraft silhouette, route and furnishings to the builder. The web-novel continuity independently pairs Velgrynd with Emperor Rudra. [Web-novel character reference](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/WN:Velgrynd)

The template measures **89 × 52 × 85** (x/y/z). Its south anchor is local (44, 5, 78). The survey can move the trailhead by 48 blocks. A twelve-block margin eases the lower approach into the surrounding ground. All floor candidates inside the gallery are checked for block light. Exterior cliffs remain natural terrain.

The inventory covered the 77 pinned jars, including structures, entity assets and world-generation features. The existing Dragon’s Nest uses a snowy woven roost, while Luminous’s Sacred Mountain has a white sealed gate. Here the rock and gallery are freshly modelled. Shared noise and lighting helpers are reused from the repository. No third-party NBT, art or entities are included.
