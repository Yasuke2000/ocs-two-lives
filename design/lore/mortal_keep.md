# Ruined Border Keep

The outer bailey is ringed by an earthen bank and shallow ditch. Broken palisades cover its exposed flank. At its southern entrance, two stone markers lead past the remount pen and the roofed issue counter. A cart track passes between the stumps of a gatehouse. Beyond it, the eastern curtain has fallen into the ward. The northwest corner still stands two storeys high. Enter through the opening in its south wall and climb the timber lookout ladder to see the breach from above. Two high slits in the surviving north wall catch the sky. A single worn stair and broken jamb mark the floor of a lost guard room.

The template measures 75 × 22 × 73 blocks (east–west, height, north–south). The southern approach is surveyed from (−670, −520) in the Mortal Realm. Its three-block gate and broken eastern curtain give walkers two ways out. The survey chooses dry land and the placer cuts or skirts the edge to meet it.

**Our interpretation.** Norham Castle defended a border crossing above the Tweed. Its surviving keep suggested the contrast between a tall fragment and a much lower enclosure. The gate, missing guard room and timber platform are an original ruin for Life 1. [Norham Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norham_Castle)
