# Ballachia — The Freed Travellers' Yard

Masayuki's party helps expose Orthrus in the small western Kingdom of Ballachia. After the
raid, the freed elves need safe passage home to the Great Jura Forest. The yard depicts the
morning of their departure. The manga account places the raid in Ballachia, and the anime
gives the event from Rimuru's side as Soei brings him the news. [Chapter 108, *The Eve of the Festival*](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/The_Eve_of_the_Festival) · [Episode 65, *The Lightspeed Hero*](https://www.crunchyroll.com/watch/G50UZ0PKJ/the-lightspeed-hero) · [Orthrus](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Orthrus)

**Our interpretation.** Under the brick roof of the former exchange, the fronts of the six
bays stand open. Short lengths of iron grille survive between them. Provisions and folded
blankets occupy the hall. Two parted iron-grey chain links in the paving mark the walk from
the arcade to the cart shed. From here the escort can follow the cobbled road east. The
hall, lodging house, cart, memorial and slate-roofed town bell are our staging of the episode.

The box is **76 × 38 × 66 blocks** (x, y, z). The south approach joins the east road at local
(38, 4, 41). The survey starts at **x −1384, z −212**, inside land region F on the current
14,000-block map. The political fan map marks Ballachia separately. The pack's coarser
grid groups its land with Falmuth. [Map research and original reference coordinates](world.md#f-kingdom-of-falmuth) · [Pack landmark registry](../../pack/config/ttl/canon_regions.json)

The hall, belfry and yard are original blockwork built with the pack's town and lighting
helpers. The mod inventories include dwarf and lizardman prisons and a dwarven merchant
cart in Tensura, as well as Iron's ravager prison. A western civilian yard called for a
different layout. Das_Felixle's town hall provided a community reference for the scale
contrast between a market and its belfry. The author lists no download or reuse licence.
All geometry was built locally. [Das_Felixle, *Medieval Town Hall*](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/medieval-town-hall-2471489/)
