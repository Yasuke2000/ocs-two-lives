# Golden City of El Dorado — Leon’s audience court

## What the sources show

In episode 87, Laplace and his companions pose as eastern merchants to meet Leon in **Golden City El Dorado**. The episode synopsis names the place. The wide shot shows three visitors facing broad white stairs with a red runner edged in gold. Gold balustrades flank the ascent. Leon's high-backed throne has a pointed outline and purple-red upholstery against tall pale wall panels. A second view shows the thick gilded armrests. In another still Leon stands under glazed ribs beside climbing roses and an ornate mirror; the image does not establish how that space connects to the audience hall. The Japanese series encyclopedia's geography entry describes a six-pointed city on magically levelled land, with streets spiralling upward towards a white royal castle. The city plan forms a layered defensive spell. The entry cites the volume 13.5 setting guide (p. 210) and light novel volume 18 (pp. 123–125); those passages were consulted through the encyclopedia. The pack atlas places the continent to the south-west and links its wealth to a volcano. The volcano and its mines lie beyond this build. ## The court and our interpretation

Two gold-tipped obelisks frame the southern road. At the crossing between the reflecting pools, two triangles in the paving form a six-pointed star as a small reminder of the city plan. Short bridges lead west and east to arcades with white columns and dark green hipped roofs. The formal paving gives way to a narrow dirt track that bends past the obelisks. Patches of coarse dirt and short grass follow the foot of the arcades and the orchard planters. The approach, pools and trees are our interpretation.

Inside the white-walled hall, a red and gold aisle climbs five shallow steps to the throne. White stone flanks the runner, and low gold rails follow the outside edges. A peaked gold frame rises behind the purple backrest. These are the features taken from the audience shot. Our interpretation supplies the dimensions, the upper lantern of amber glass and copper, the benches, and the octagonal footprint. The surrounding spiral streets and their defences are outside the palace precinct.

At the north end of the **east arcade**, the roof changes from dark prismarine to glass between gold ribs. Follow the rose beds to a freestanding mirror: blue glass over a lavender backing gives the oval depth, and the white frame widens into two carved feet. From the central aisle the glass catches the light between the trellises. Episode 87 shows the oval, scrollwork and pink and white flowers against gold-traced glazing. Episode 89 also pictures Leon speaking with Elmesia beside flowers and a carved white upright. These images inform the materials and the scale of the details. Placing that corner beside the audience court is our interpretation. The mirror is decorative. [Episode 87, garden and mirror](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/anime/tensura-portal-anime-tensura/assets/images/story/no92/img3.jpg) · [Episode 89, audience with Elmesia](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no94) · [Episode 89, floral detail](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/anime/tensura-portal-anime-tensura/assets/images/story/no94/img5.jpg)

On the dais, two small pointed gold ornaments sit against the pale wall panels on either side of the throne. Their profiles echo the isolated pendants visible beyond the visitors in the wide shot. ## Scale and placement

The blueprint is **81 × 39 × 83** blocks (x, y, z). The obelisk approach at local **(40, 4, 74)** is anchored at world **x −4808, z 3240** before terrain survey. The three structure slabs start at z 0, 28 and 56. Including the 48-block search radius and 16-block terrain margin, the bounding rectangle is x −4912 through −4704, z 3102 through 3312. All 210 chunks it intersects are marked X. The older 10,000-wide coordinate in the lore atlas falls at sea on this map. The current 14,000-wide landmark converts to (−4805, 3241), three blocks east and one south of this explicit anchor. [Placement](../builds/el_dorado.toml) · [Political fan-map trace](../../pack/config/ttl/canon_map.txt)

The blueprint supplies paving, pool beds and a single surface layer for the irregular turf verge. The placer fills gaps below these surfaces with locally selected earth; the region’s wooded badlands and savanna therefore do not acquire a buried sandstone platform. At the box perimeter, the 16-block margin grades cuts and fills at one block per block of distance. The placer can still trim high ground or tree crowns above the survey height in unbuilt columns inside the box. The final approach slope depends on the in-game survey.
