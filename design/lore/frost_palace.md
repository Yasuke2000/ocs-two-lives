# Frost Palace — the southern gate

Guy Crimson kept his castle encased in ice after his encounter with Velzard. The cold across the Ice Continent comes from the Frost Dragon herself. That detail guided the entrance: a strip of white masonry is visible beneath the blue ice on each gate tower. See Fuse's [volume 16, epilogue “Guy Crimson”](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975369750-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-16-light-novel), and the wiki entries for the [Frost Palace](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Frost_Palace) and the [Ice Continent](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Continent).

**Our interpretation.** The landmark occupies 85 blocks east–west by 69 north–south, in a 51-block-high box. A low quartz bridge crosses a winding blue seam in the ice. From the south its rail lamps frame the red lozenge above the gateway. Two great faceted gate towers carry the highest lights; shorter towers mark the landward end. Between them a crimson runner leads through six ribs of a glazed vault and out onto the north terrace. The seam, vault, runner, heraldry and floor plan are our design.

**Placement.** The walking anchor is blueprint (42, 4, 65), near the foot of the south causeway. The survey starts at world (−2435, −4314). At the current map width the political-map pixel (1345, 349) already lands on Ice Continent. The entire footprint, plus its 8-block skirt and 48-block survey radius, lies within cells marked `I`. The map's biome paint describes the land; local slope, snow and the placed bridge still need a server visit. [Pack geography and fan-map provenance](world.md)

**Build references.** The pack's existing throne-room and goblin-village NBTs serve other locations. The installed Tensura mod's Charybdis ice entrance suggested the blue-ice, packed-ice and snow palette. Iron's Spells' citadel entrance tower and Towns and Towers' snowy-plains outpost were checked for scale. We also consulted Wicked Builders' frozen-sea palace as a community reference. The blueprint uses the pack’s drawing and lighting helpers; all of its block geometry is original. [Wicked Builders, *Ice palace*](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/ice-palace-4610917/)
