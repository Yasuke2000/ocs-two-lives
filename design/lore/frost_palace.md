# Frost Palace — the southern gate

Guy Crimson kept his castle encased in ice after his encounter with Velzard. The cold across the Ice Continent comes from the Frost Dragon herself. That detail guided the entrance: a strip of white masonry is visible beneath the blue ice on each gate tower. See Fuse's [volume 16, epilogue “Guy Crimson”](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975369750-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-16-light-novel), and the wiki entries for the [Frost Palace](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Frost_Palace) and the [Ice Continent](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Continent). The Japanese series wiki’s Ice Continent entry traces the palace’s ice covering to the novels. [Ice Continent geography](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/転生したらスライムだった件#氷土の大陸)

**What Leon sees.** In anime episode 42, “The Demon Lords,” curved blades of ice frame the first distant view of Guy's castle. Leon walks down a dark blue passage with pointed wall bays, clusters of small crystals at their feet and a reflective floor. Beyond the guarded doors the hall opens into a bright space with blue lines dividing the floor panels and violet lights raised above the crowd. The official gallery shows Leon and Velzard against that blue interior. The entrance build follows the outer ice silhouette and the passage, ending before Guy's reception hall. [Official episode 42 gallery, third and fourth stills](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no45) · [Episode 42 frame sequence, shots 40–45](https://anison.fc2.net/blog-entry-183.html)

**At the audience door.** In Fuse's web novel Leon follows the palace corridor to a large carved door. Two demons open it together; an attendant on the far side announces his arrival. The anime gives that moment a silhouette a visitor can read from down the aisle: patterned dark-blue leaves and a bright opening between them. In the build, each leaf sits ajar on a deepslate jamb. Pale ice curls across its blue panels above a dark grip. The leaves leave five blocks of walking width between their inner edges. Two violet cup lamps on quartz stems mark the final bay. Their amethyst tips, the pattern of the carving and the stationary door angle are our interpretation. [Fuse's web novel, chapter 78](https://ncode.syosetu.com/n6316bn/81/) · [Episode 42, “The Demon Lords”](https://www.crunchyroll.com/watch/GWDU8EWD8/the-demon-lords)

Leon's visit is also told in the interlude of light novel volume 6 and the manga's chapter 76, “Demon Lord and True Dragon.” The official world map identifies the White Ice Palace on the frozen continent and records Guy's named demon servants. [Yen Press, volume 6](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975301194-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-6-light-novel) · [Kodansha, chapter 76](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/title/00380/episode/312462) · [Official map, White Ice Palace](https://www.ten-sura.com/map/#A-130)

**Our interpretation.** The landmark occupies 85 blocks east–west by 69 north–south, in a 51-block-high box. Curving ice spires draw the eye across a quartz bridge toward two faceted gate towers. A visitor passes under a backlit violet lozenge, walks between dark blue lines in a packed-ice floor and comes out onto the north terrace. Small crystal clusters sit at the bases of the glazed bays. The bridge across the frozen cleft, the compass in its deck, the tower count, the violet arch ornament and the spacing of the vault ribs are our architectural choices.

**Placement.** The walking anchor is blueprint (42, 4, 65), near the foot of the south causeway. The survey starts at world (−2435, −4314). At the current map width the political-map pixel (1345, 349) already lands on Ice Continent. The entire footprint, plus its 8-block skirt and 48-block survey radius, lies within cells marked `I`. The map's biome paint describes the land; local slope, snow and the placed bridge still need a server visit. [Pack geography and fan-map provenance](world.md)

**Meeting the snowfield.** From the south, a three-block track of compacted snow and exposed gravel widens into the causeway steps. Wind-packed ridges skirt the path; both ends of the blue ice seam narrow and disappear under the banks. The thinner outer snow shelf has an irregular outline. The placer keeps the approach open by grading eight blocks beyond the box. In snow biomes it overlays raised ground there with grass over dirt; the white drifts and path are authored inside that boundary. It can also cut high ground in the empty corners. Check the uphill and downhill approaches after the server survey. [Placer terrain rules](../../pack/kubejs/server_scripts/ttl/life/builds.js)

**Build references.** The pack's existing throne-room and goblin-village NBTs serve other locations. The installed Tensura mod's Charybdis ice entrance suggested the blue-ice, packed-ice and snow palette. Iron's Spells' citadel entrance tower and Towns and Towers' snowy-plains outpost were checked for scale. We also consulted Wicked Builders' frozen-sea palace as a community reference. The blueprint uses the pack’s drawing and lighting helpers; all of its block geometry is original. [Wicked Builders, *Ice palace*](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/ice-palace-4610917/)
