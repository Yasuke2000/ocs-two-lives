# Amrita — Buried Gallery of Jistav

## Current source/placement correction — 20 November 2026

Official anime episode81 (route no86) explicitly places the ancient Amrita ruins beneath Clayman's former castle. LN10 separately identifies Soma as the older destroyed homeland and Amrita as the later distant refuge-city. The public mound/shaft described below is the existing server blueprint interpretation, not a source-established canonical entrance. The current authored map keeps a separate game reservation(3800,-500), radius63, because the unchanged rule requires E350..700 from the castle(3300,-600). An inert castle-linked access projection is drawn for C07 review; depth, entrance coordinate and full underground extent remain unverified. Eastern chambers are possible. No runtime or placement change is made. ## Historical blueprint interpretation — not canonical access

A grassy mound rises over the old capital. Broken column bases surround the open shaft. Between two stone wardens, a flight of steps descends under three surviving lintels. The vaulted nave leads past eight memorial alcoves to a sealed door set with luminous joints. The oculus lets daylight reach the patterned floor. Its parapet marks the drop on the hill above.

Jistav was Clayman's domain and home to dark elves. Amrita is both the capital and a subterranean ancient ruin. The Japanese setting summary, citing Fuse's light novel volume 10, pages 15–16, describes Kazalim and his people modelling it on Soma and giving it magical defences including golems. **Our interpretation** is a single surviving public gallery. The carved sentinels represent the golems in stone. The oculus, blue-grey tuff ribs, memorial niches, stair and copper-inlaid sealed door are original. The statues have no entities, traps or encounter logic.
