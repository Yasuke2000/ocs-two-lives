## Source-qualified setting
Fandom answered our fetch tool with HTTP 402, so these come from search snippets plus `design/lore/world.md` section L.
- Lubelius is the theocratic headquarters of the Western Holy Church, the Luminous faith's holy land; the wiki compares
  it to the Catholic Church and Vatican City. Its capital Lune is "one of the most well developed human cities ever
  built", with high-end magic and technology. (https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_Empire_of_Lubelius,
  https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Holy_Church)
- The Sacred Mountain is the holiest site with an Inner Sanctum on top, guarded by the Church, the Empire's forces
  and secretly by Luminous's vampire servants. Holy Emperor Louis Valentin is secretly a vampire serving Luminous.
- Under the holy city lies the Night Garden (world.md). Hinata Sakaguchi leads the Temple Knights.
- A fan-translated web novel chapter has Hinata guiding visitors into a lantern-lit, bustling capital at night
  (unverified, secondary). WN:Holy_City_Lubelius (listed in world.md) was not readable.

## Our interpretation (canon gives no architecture)
White and gold, a Vatican-like cathedral district, as the owner asked.
- **Holy Gate** (z 101-109): white wall, two gold-capped pylons with spires, a 9 wide passage with hanging lanterns,
  light blue banners. Arrival point blueprint (60, 5, 114).
- **Holy Square** (z 64-102): a paved disc of quartz, calcite and smooth quartz with gold and chiseled rings, a fountain
  round a golden-crowned pillar (top y 25), eight braziers, rose gardens (Luminous's roses), benches.
- **Cathedral** (z 8-62): cross-shaped, white quartz bricks and pillars with light blue stained glass; nave x 49-71, transept
  x 30-90, four gabled arms of smooth quartz, a drum with windows and a hemispherical dome (radius 11, gold bands, lantern
  turret, rod to y 55) over the crossing, twin facade towers (x 40-48, 72-80; open belfries with bells, spires to y 58),
  a rose window and a seven wide portal in the facade. Interior: marble floor with gold inlay, red carpet, pews in the south
  arm, a raised choir with a gold altar in the north arm, long chandeliers, candelabra.
- **Hall of the Holy Knights** (east, x 93-111) with a colonnade, a white shield with a gold cross, long tables and banners
  (Hinata's Temple Knights); mirrored **Pilgrims' Hall** (west) with white banners.
The Night Garden (below) and the Sacred Mountain approach are separate pieces and not part of this build.
