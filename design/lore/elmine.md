# Elmine — Terrace of the Divine Tree

A pale staircase rises between the buttress roots to a birch promenade around the trunk. Two copper-capped reading pavilions stand out over the grove. Walk through the opening between them to find the charter room *inside* the wood, with bookshelves and a lectern. Above the terrace, nine great limbs divide again beneath the crown.

The Divine Tree houses Elmine both inside and upon its trunk. It rivals mountains in the setting. The anime glossary describes Sarion as a power comparable with Dwargon, and the setting describes thirteen elven royal houses under the dynasty. The thirteen pairs of green inlays on the promenade represent those houses. **Our interpretation** is a public embassy terrace beneath a compressed crown. The charter room, colours, pavilions, inlays and walkable stair are original designs. The **75 × 60 × 75** template includes the south approach and three north–south slabs. Its walking anchor is local **(37, 4, 67)**. On Lerfing's map Elmine stands at world **(1100, 2760)** in S, on the terrace beside **Paulzero's giant tree** (his grove spans x 350..1050, z 2600..3150), the one literal Divine Tree on the map (plains, relief 3). Placement surveys up to 32 blocks around it. Real slopes, trunk-to-terrace sightlines and the approach through the generated forest await server inspection. [Political map, second plate](https://i.redd.it/q1p4ksudqyah1.png)

The pack's Treyni shrine represents a Jura dryad sanctuary. Tensura: Reincarnated's ancient-forest tree templates are authored for that forest. Its pinned jar and the other mod inventories contain no named Elmine landmark. *Siofra the Elven Village* provides a community comparison for settlements set among giant trees. No external structure, layout, image or geometry was copied. Elmine uses the pack's shared lighting and geometry code and original vanilla blockwork.
