# Elmine — Terrace of the Divine Tree

Elmine is represented by a public terrace in a colossal living tree. The [indexed Divine Tree article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Tree), Appearance, describes the capital within and on its trunk, citing LN6 chapter 2 and anime episode 61. Those specific primary passages are not directly verified here. The [pinned secondary panorama](https://git.fish/KartoffelChipss/TensuraMap/src/commit/ec4d0e3db2ba4b229d412a273bbcdd19f45425b6/public/img/locations/elmine.webp) supplies visual context; exact episode/timestamp remains unverified. Relic origin, thirteen royal houses and exact scale require their own primary corroboration.

[Official episode 61](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no65), stills 2 and 5, supplies a narrower primary visual reference: pale botanical columns, open-air greenery, pale seating and a round table in the silver-haired elven character's pavilion scene. These images do not establish a complete city plan, its position in the tree or its S1E10 condition.

**Our interpretation:** the existing 75×60×75 lower-terrace blueprint has a pale root staircase, birch promenade, two copper-capped reading pavilions, a charter room, green inlays and compressed crown. Rooms, colours, branch count and decorative symbolism are original choices. This small precinct does not represent the complete tree or capital.
