# Heaven Tower, Holy Void of Damargania

Dagruel rules a country of giants in the far west. The pack's geography notes identify Heaven Tower as its capital and place it in the Desert of Death, where the ancient battle between Guy and Milim laid waste to fertile ground. The pale tower rises alone above sand and exposed bands of hardpan. [Pack geography and fan-map sources](world.md#v-holy-void-of-damargania-聖虚ダマルガニア) · [Holy Void of Damargania](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_Void_of_Damargania) · [Dagruel](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Dagruel) · [Heaven Tower](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Heaven_Tower)

## The climb

Our interpretation gives the giants a procession road with two pairs of gate stones. The inner pair rises twice as high as the outer pair. From the road, a visitor can see the tower's crown through its silhouette. Inside the great south doorway, a double diamond set into the paving lies under an open shaft. A ladder beside it serves the galleries at y 19, 33, 44 and 48. Leaning ribs carry the successive octagonal terraces. An empty stone doorway on the summit repeats the outline of the entrance below.

The tower's section, colours, dry landscape, crown and route through the galleries are our interpretation. The wiki pages were unavailable to direct requests during this build. The setting follows the pack's sourced geography notes. None of the cited material establishes a floor plan or the shape of the crown.

## Placement

The blueprint spans 81 × 60 × 91 blocks (x, y, z). Its anchor is the walking cell (40, 4, 83), looking north from the road. The current fan-map pixel (414, 1186) converts to **x −7507, z 245** on Damargania land (`V`). The terrain survey can move the anchor within 48 blocks.
