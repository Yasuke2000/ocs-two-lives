# Heaven Tower, Holy Void of Damargania

In Fuse's light novel, Rimuru visits Damargania with Ultima to install a transfer circle. He describes a great tower rising above the wasteland. The ground around it, inside an ancient barrier, provides refuge even for Dagruel's giants. Beyond it, corrosive sandstorms persist after Guy and Milim's battle. The **Holy Void** is this sheltered area around Heaven Tower (天通閣). The pack uses the tower as the capital's map marker. [Volume 18, chapter 2, GC Novels](https://gcnovels.jp/book/1164) · [Japanese series wiki, Damargania, citing vol. 18 pp. 106–109](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/転生したらスライムだった件#ダマルガニア) · [Pack geography](world.md#v-holy-void-of-damargania-聖虚ダマルガニア)

Dagruel guards a route through the tower to the Heavenly Star Palace. Volume 19's interlude explains who can use the other entrance, and chapter 2 describes the former divine fortress, the opening above it and movement between its upper and lower levels. In chapter 3, Rimuru faces Michael inside a hall of the tower. The empty frame at its summit represents that passage in stone. [Volume 19, interlude and chapters 2–3, GC Novels](https://gcnovels.jp/book/1245)

## The climb

Our interpretation makes the protected ground readable on foot. Red seams of sand end at an octagonal curb around a pale court. Four low markers carry the line around its corners. These are architectural features. The south road passes between two pairs of gate stones, with the taller pair nearest the entrance. A double diamond in the paving marks the foot of the open shaft. A ladder beside it serves the galleries at y 19, 33, 44 and 48. Two arcades of dark piers divide the ground hall into an open central aisle and side aisles with sandstone seats. From the entrance, a visitor can see a dark recess shaped like the summit door, with an amber keystone above it. On the first gallery the south doorway leads out under a rust-edged canopy. The guard can look down the full length of the processional road. The hall and the upper-floor vantage follow the novel. The piers, seats, blind doorway and roofed porch are our interpretation.

Our interpretation also supplies the stepped octagonal silhouette, cut sandstone, rust-coloured bands and dark ribs. The cited passages establish the tower's great height, its setting and its function. A labelled exterior view or an exterior colour could not be verified in the illustrations reviewed for volumes 18–20. The official anime portrait identifies Dagruel; the published episode 46 and 48 stills show the Walpurgis storyline. None supplies a tower elevation. ## Placement

The blueprint spans 81 × 60 × 91 blocks (x, y, z). Its anchor is the walking cell (40, 4, 83), looking north from the road. The current fan-map pixel (414, 1186) converts to **x −7507, z 245** on Damargania land (`V`). The terrain survey can move the anchor within 48 blocks. On the south approach, windblown sand covers first the road borders and then the paving beyond the arrival stone. At the perimeter, sandstone underlies low tongues of sand. The paving disappears before the southern edge, where an eleven-block-wide sand bed remains level with the arrival stone. The placer clears high ground across the surveyed box and uses a sixteen-block margin to cut or fill the approach toward the surrounding desert at one block per block.
