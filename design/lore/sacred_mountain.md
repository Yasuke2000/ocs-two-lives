# Sacred Mountain — the sealed gate of the Inner Sanctum

A pilgrim approaching from the south can see the blue and gold seal between the limestone peaks before setting foot on the first stair. The lower flight climbs seven blocks to a rose court. The second climbs eight to the threshold, where three courses of white stone and gold frame dark double leaves. A single gold ring spans their seam. The timber line ends below the gate.

## Canon and sources

In Fuse's web novel, chapter 87, Hinata reaches a door deep inside the Great Cathedral and opens it. The Inner Sanctum (*Oku no In*) guards the approach to the Holy Emperor’s residence. Hinata follows a mountain path to meet her teachers at a house halfway up the slope. The passage gives the procession a direction and a guarded threshold. It leaves the door’s material, shape and decoration unspecified. The Japanese synopsis of light novel volume 6 also places Laplace’s encounter with Valentine inside Lubelius’s Inner Sanctum. [Fuse, “Holy City,” paragraphs 100–110](https://ncode.syosetu.com/n6316bn/91/#L100) · [Volume 6 synopsis](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/転生したらスライムだった件#あらすじ6巻)

In the season 3 anime, episode 50 places Louis and Hinata in council in Lubelius when Luminous returns. The fifth gallery still shows Louis in white and gold beside Hinata. Episode 53 places the joint meeting of the Crusaders and Imperial Guard in Lubelius. Its third still shows the assembled knights and its fourth shows Luminous against dark red and gold. These views establish a ceremonial palette. Neither gallery identifies the exterior of the Sacred Mountain gate. The official map describes Lubelius as the Western Holy Church’s holy land and headquarters. [Season 3, episode 50 gallery](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no54) · [Season 3, episode 53 gallery](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no57) · [Official map, Lubelius](https://www.ten-sura.com/map/#A-110) · [Volume 7 publisher’s synopsis](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975301200-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-7-light-novel)

Hinata names the Seven Luminaries as her teachers on the approach. When she reaches the meeting house, four of the seven are present. The anime’s character illustration shows a member of the clergy with a covered face, a gold circlet, pale grey cloth on either side of a white robe and small red clasps. That clothing supplied the reference for the carvings. Louis’s official portrait and the council stills depict the people and court of Lubelius. None of these pictures depicts this temple façade. ## Our interpretation

The 81 × 57 × 81 block volume (x, y, z) holds a single ascent and its rock face. Starting at blueprint (40, 4, 76), visitors walk north through two lantern-lined flights. The ledges narrow behind the gate and separate into weathered rock shoulders. The exterior door is a solid seal for this landmark. Visitors can stand at the threshold; Hinata opens the cathedral door in the web novel. The rose courts, glass medallion, paired pinnacles and stair layout are our interpretation. Quartz, calcite and gold connect the sanctuary visually to [the cathedral at Lune](lubelius.md).

At the upper landing, seven lights trace an arc between the visitor and the seal. Each is flush with the paving, with a gold edge toward the door and a chiseled stone toward the steps. Their count recalls Hinata’s teachers. Two shallow, dark-backed niches hold veiled reliefs. A visitor standing beside a pier can see the separate folds of the robe, the gold circlet and the small red clasp below the veil. Across the door itself, copper straps finish at black studs, with a second pair of studs at hand height beside the seam. These floor sockets, carvings and fittings are our interpretation of the guarded threshold.

At the southern boundary the steps reach a level apron. Marble gives way to broken paving, gravel and a narrow earth path at the surveyed walking height. Long grassy spurs flank the stair; the rock rises behind them and thins to an earth lip along the perimeter. The placer fills underneath this lip from the existing ground and permits a one-block rise or fall per block of distance in the 16-block margin. The survey range, footprint and margin together occupy 196 map cells, all land in Lubelius. [Terrain fitting code](../../pack/kubejs/server_scripts/ttl/life/builds.js)

Three 27-block-deep structure slabs join along z. Ground survey can move the approach up to 48 blocks along each horizontal axis. The offline builder checks all spawnable surfaces for block light and checks the support of plants and lanterns. The actual terrain fit, visibility from the path and integrity of the seal after server placement need an in-game visit.
