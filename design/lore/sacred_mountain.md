# Sacred Mountain — the sealed gate of the Inner Sanctum

A pilgrim approaching from the south can see the blue and gold seal between the limestone peaks before setting foot on the first stair. The lower flight climbs seven blocks to a rose court. The second climbs eight to the threshold, where three courses of white stone and gold frame dark double leaves. A single gold ring spans their seam. The timber line ends below the gate.

## Canon and sources

The Sacred Mountain is the holy site of Lubelius, with the Inner Sanctum at its summit. The pack places it northwest of Lune, distinct from the cathedral in the capital. The political fan map traced in the project puts the mountain at reference pixel (980, 905). At the current map width this is world x −4423, z −1286. The project’s geography notes preserve the wiki research; the wiki returned HTTP 402 during this build. [World notes, region L](world.md) · [Holy Empire of Lubelius](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_Empire_of_Lubelius)

Hinata serves as chief knight of Lubelius's Imperial Guard and captain of its Crusader forces. The publisher's volume 7 synopsis establishes that military context. It gives no design for the entrance. [Fuse, *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*, volume 7, Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975301200-that-time-i-got-reincarnated-as-a-slime-vol-7-light-novel)

## Our interpretation

The 81 × 57 × 81 block volume (x, y, z) holds a single ascent and its rock face. Starting at blueprint (40, 4, 76), visitors walk north through two lantern-lined flights. The ledges narrow behind the gate and separate into weathered rock shoulders. The door is a solid architectural seal. The rose courts, glass medallion, paired pinnacles and stair layout are our interpretation. Quartz, calcite and gold connect the sanctuary visually to [the cathedral at Lune](lubelius.md).

Three 27-block-deep structure slabs join along z. Ground survey can move the approach up to 48 blocks along each horizontal axis. The offline builder checks all spawnable surfaces for block light and checks the support of plants and lanterns. The actual terrain fit, visibility from the path and integrity of the seal after server placement need an in-game visit.
