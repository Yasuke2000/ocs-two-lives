# Morgdahl — The Timber Landing

The cart road crosses the millrace on its way into the yard. From the bridge, look west to see a twelve-paddle oak wheel in front of the white-plastered mill. Its axle passes through the south wall toward a framed saw and sliding log carriage. Upstream stands a raised sluice, downstream a collecting pool. Across the water, a jib suspends a single trunk above the trestles. Birch stock dries under a separate shelter. The machinery is static blockwork.

The council map in season four identifies Morgdahl among the small western countries. Taiqi_’s frame comparison shows its relative location. The pack takes the exact point from Tinseluwu’s political map. The pack’s region O includes plains, forest, birch and swamp, with the subtitle “A river-mouth realm on the southern coast of the Western Nations.” The grounded timber industry, mill buildings and local watercourse are **our interpretation**. The published sources give no street plan or principal industry.
