# Raja’s old lakeside workings

From the landing, visitors can see the winding wheel against a grey ridge. Follow the rails under the portal to the back of the mine. A side gallery branches west behind the timber sets. Outside, two settling beds stand on the shore. Across the yard, an assay house has sample benches and a small crown in its south window. The template is 80 × 37 × 70 blocks. Its walking anchor at local (36, 6, 49) places the haul road near world (−1084, 763).

The survey searches up to 48 blocks along either axis, all within Raja on the pack map, and tolerates up to 8% naturally wet samples. The inlet itself is part of the template.

In *Scarlet Bond*, Queen Towa rules Raja, a small state west of Tempest. The fortunes of its gold mines and the contamination of the lake are tied to the queen’s inherited tiara. The franchise credits miners in Towa’s service among the film’s characters. The political cartographer used by the pack describes Raja’s precise border position as speculative. **Our interpretation** shows a quiet stretch of shoreline after the crisis. The clear inlet, exhausted galleries, winding tower, two stone washing beds and shore house make a walkable cross-section of the former industry. At the surface a cable links the timber sheave to its drum. The crown window is an invented local tribute to Towa. The mine furniture, water and window are ordinary vanilla blocks. The National Trust’s record of Dolaucothi describes adits, water channels for washing ore and a wooden headframe from a later period. Those distinct features informed our mine yard. [History of Dolaucothi](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/wales/dolaucothi/history-of-dolaucothi)
