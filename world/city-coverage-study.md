# Dwargon and Englassia — city coverage study

This is a **non-geographical scope diagram**. Box positions, grouping and sizes are page layout, not terrain, compass bearings, street coordinates, rooms, routes or tunnels. Nothing in it registers or places content. Production remains the same 58-site map.

## Dwargon

The [indexed Dwargon article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Armed_Nation_of_Dwargon) names three major cities: Central, East and West, with Central as capital (WIK-DWG-002). It also locates Butterflies of the Night in Dwargon (WIK-DWG-003). Those claims remain `fan-wiki`: cited novels are research leads, not independently read passages. Names alone do not prove exact compass placement or a tunnel network.

The active `dwargon` and `dwargo` anchors reserve compressed server terrain for the gate/inner-capital context. Neither the small gate footprint nor a capital marker proves that all three cities, their districts and venues have been reconstructed or fit. East and West remain missing city representations. The elf venue already has authored design evidence in [dwargon.py](../blueprints/dwargon.py): the header and material definition explicitly name the Night Butterflies cabaret. Existing dwargon_0..2 NBT files are non-empty. This is implementation presence, not verified canon architecture or placement on the new map. No extra entrance or settlement coordinate is invented.

## Englassia

The [Free Guild article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Free_Guild) places the main headquarters within the capital Lura (WIK-GUI-002). Its academy section describes the institution's guild association. The [Council article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_the_West) places the Council in Englassia and distinguishes that country from Jura-bordering members (WIK-COW-001). **Country membership is not a verified Lura parcel.** No exact assembly building, street or campus plan follows from those summaries.

The [official episode20 synopsis](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no20) directly supports Shizu teaching summoned children in Englassia (OFF-EDU-001). It does not name the campus or supply coordinates. The later visit is distinct from the selected initial S1E10 instant. Exact school buildings, residents and condition require their own source/era review.

The `englassia` reservation is a capital terrain slot. [englassia.py](../blueprints/englassia.py) already defines guild() and academy(), and its registry describes both. Non-empty englassia_0..2 NBT files exist. Reuse those designs after source/scale/era review; do not call them absent merely because they lack separate SVG circles. Council premises and the royal castle still lack distinct representation in this audit. None of this proves completed runtime placement. The castle's lake setting has a separately qualified WN reference (C17); that evidence does not authorize flooding the current dry slot or silently merging continuities.

## Consequence for the map goal

All seven city-related follow-ups remain open: three have existing authored designs (elf venue, guild and academy), while four remain missing distinct representations. The [local evidence manifest](../docs/research/tensura-world-2026-11-19/city-design-evidence.csv) records file hashes and its verification limits. The [126-entry scope audit](../docs/research/tensura-world-2026-11-19/mainland-coverage-audit.md) keeps these distinct from unlocated countries such as Doran, from offshore places, and from other worlds. A source-backed parent country is useful design context; it is not equivalent to completed internal geography or runtime acceptance.

The production SVG, current terrain build and existing preview are unchanged in this documentation pass. No technical scripts, generated map data, new IDs, runtime placement or deployment are changed.
