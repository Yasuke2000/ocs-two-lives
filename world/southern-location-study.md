## Filtwood: country identity versus the U binding

The [official episode 24 synopsis](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no24) identifies Filtwood as a kingdom visited by Shizu and explicitly places the story before Rimuru reincarnates (OFF-FIL-001/002). Its high episode number does not make the country or the flashback events later than the selected Kijin snapshot.

The [wiki article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_of_Filtwood) puts it near/bordering Jura (WIK-FIL-001). This is secondary geographical evidence, with no reliable compass side or exact capital supplied. The old creative pack's statement that it is a canonical town in Ulgracia was unsupported. The current server anchor at `(-1200,2750)` is required in gameplay U by `design/data/anchors.toml`; that binding is not a verified political relationship. The anchor and its footprint remain fixed, and the atlas now makes the dispute visible.

The drawn trial circle at `(-1050,600)`, radius 75, is **our interpretation** of one possible Jura-edge placement. Its western side, radius and identity as any specific settlement are not canon facts. It is an alternative for resolving C20, not an approved move. A final placement needs stronger source evidence and reconciliation of the U binding. No technical rule or data file is changed.

All five official episode 24 stills were inspected. [Still3](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/anime/tensura-portal-anime-tensura/assets/images/story/no24/img3.jpg) and [still4](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/anime/tensura-portal-anime-tensura/assets/images/story/no24/img4.jpg) show an ornate interior with adventurers; the others focus on characters. They supply no exterior town plan, coastline, route or surveyed bearing. Only links are stored here. Neither the synopsis nor these stills independently establishes which flashback residents/buildings survive at S1E10.

## Orbic: the homeland is distinct from the battlefield

The [publisher-hosted LN2 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime2.htm) was reread: depleted land and famine push the orcs toward a forest, with three demon-lord territories around the homeland. That passage does not name the rulers or give cardinal bearings; the existing LN claims retain that limit.

The [Orbic wiki article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Orc_Kingdom_of_Orbic) places the homeland south of Jura, north of Fulbrosia and Eurazania, and west of Milim's lands (WIK-ORB-001). The eight-vertex vicinity around `(800,1120)` follows those broad relationships in this authored map. Its extent and vertices are **our interpretation**, not a country survey or a source-confirmed capital. It is south of the current Orc War battlefield reservation and remains separately drawn.

At the chosen initial era the Orc Lord conflict is unresolved. This review depicts a depleted origin, without inventing an intact capital, numbered villages, a dimensional rift footprint or a contemporary thriving kingdom. No new site ID is added; C15 registration and terrain treatment remain open. The wiki inventory therefore still marks Orbic missing in active production, despite having a review proposal.
