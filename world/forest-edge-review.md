# Eastern forest edge review — round22

[Before/after vector study](forest-edge-study.png) · [editable study](forest-edge-study.svg) · [measurements and raster audit](forest-edge-review.json).

Comparing the current terrain preview with the first current-geometry painting exposed three overly regular woodland edges: the Jistav oval, the northern Imperial patch and the long eastern Jura foothill ribbon. The [official map](https://www.ten-sura.com/map) and [official base illustration](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/assets/images/portal-map/world/intro/map-base.webp) were reinspected for broad landscape character. They do not provide surveyed vegetation boundaries. This revision is **our interpretation**, recorded as DES-FOR-022, rather than a new canonical acreage claim.

Only the point lists of three existing forest polygons change. Broad manually placed bays and lobes soften their edges; a conservative blend toward the previous perimeter limits the extent. No random noise or procedural edge seed is used. Existing forest types, all58 sites/radii, watercourses, terrain heights, roads, biomes-layer polygons and gameplay regions remain unchanged. The ancient/dark core and Treyni oldwood are untouched.

The study and selected checker/terrain previews were visually reviewed.
