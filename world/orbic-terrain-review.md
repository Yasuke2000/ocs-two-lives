## Evidence and initial state

The [publisher-hosted original LN2 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime2.htm), opening paragraphs describing exhausted land, failed crops and famine, was reread directly. It puts the homeland at a junction of three demon-lord territories and a forest (LN02-001/002). It does not supply exact boundaries, cardinal bearings, a capital plan or a Minecraft biome. The [indexed Orbic article](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Orc_Kingdom_of_Orbic) supplies the broad south-of-Jura, north-of-Fulbrosia/Eurazania and west-of-Milim relationships (WIK-ORB-001); these remain fan-wiki evidence, separate from the directly read prologue. Referenced manga chapters were not read here.

The chosen post-Kijin instant precedes the Orc Lord resolution. The origin land must be distinguished from the advancing horde and the existing `orc_camp` encounter reservation. This pass does not invent an intact royal capital, ten tribal villages, a dimensional-rift location, an active encounter or a later restored kingdom.

## Authored terrain change

`orbic-depleted-ground` is a 32-vertex rounded habitat patch within the earlier eight-vertex vicinity proposal, about239,623 square blocks, around the southern forest/foothill transition. Its exact outline and placement are **our interpretation**, not the kingdom's sovereign boundary or full historical extent. `tensura:barren_land` is an allowed project biome used as a visual/ecological proxy for exhausted ground, not a claim that the LN names that mod biome or establishes its mobs and resources. This does not transfer Orbic into western Damargania.

The overlapping northern edge of `eurazania-jungle` loses about141,942 square blocks (5.97% of that polygon), leaving about2,234,236. Other eleven forest polygons and the cultivation fringe remain unchanged. This makes the drawn canopy agree with the explicit depleted-ground habitat. The patch is a small area of the continent, away from the58 site discs and their margins; nearest site-edge clearance is about300 blocks at Treyni's shrine. No new registration, flattened town plate, gameplay mask or political border is added. An existing mandatory road passes the northern/eastern edge; it retains the era qualification in C18 and is not evidence of a canonical route through Orbic.

The official base map and fresh checker/terrain previews were visually compared. The official continental illustration does not survey Orbic's depletion boundary; this new local shape is deliberately not claimed to trace it. Broad coasts, southern gulf, eastern forest context, all58 marker positions and the other previously disclosed scale/binding differences remain. The older inactive vicinity in map.svg remains a reference envelope; it is not a second active country outline.
