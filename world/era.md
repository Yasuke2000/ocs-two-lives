# World snapshot: immediately after the Kijin naming

Yasuke selected this baseline on 20 November 2026: immediately after Rimuru names the six ogre survivors
and they evolve into Kijin. This is the naming event in **anime season 1, episode 10**, before the Orc Lord
conflict is resolved. No exact playback timestamp is claimed; the official synopsis was directly read.

[Episode 9](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no9) establishes a growing village with dwarf-built
housing. [Episode 10](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no10) describes the naming and the army
advancing against the lizardmen around Siss. Episode 11 follows the arrival of Gabiru.

## Current-state rules

- Rimuru leads a young village. Label it **Rimuru's village (future Tempest)**. Do not present the later
  federation, demon-lord state, festival metropolis, Dungeon, stadium, railway or advanced industries as built.
- Benimaru, Shuna, Shion, Souei, Hakurou and Kurobe have joined and been named. The ogre village is already
  destroyed. The Orc Lord conflict is unresolved; the orc marker is an approach/reserved encounter location,
  not a monument to a finished victory or a settled High Orc district.
- Falmuth is still **Falmuth**. Farmenas and Youm's kingship are future political changes.
- Clayman, Frey and Carrion retain Jistav, Fulbrosia and Eurazania respectively. Laura is still the
  pre-destruction capital. Milim's earlier Dragon Faithful territory is distinct from her later expanded dominion.
- The Eastern Empire is under Rudra, not Emperor Masayuki. Dwargon remains Gazel's kingdom; the continental
  kingdoms and old terrain can exist without having diplomatic ties to Rimuru yet.
- Kaijin is already helping Rimuru's village. A Dwargon workshop footprint does not make him a second
  resident there. Shizu is not an alive baseline resident. Do not prepopulate the village with later recruits
  such as Diablo, Geld's postwar population, Ramiris or the later diplomatic delegations.
- The Spirit Dwelling belongs to its earlier Ulgracia setting; its later Tempest/Dungeon association is not
  initial-state geography. Ancient ruins can already exist even when characters discover them much later.

These ownership/era consequences follow the selected story point. The chapter/episode locator supporting
the naming is exact; individual distant-country initial-state details remain qualified where primary passages
have not been read. Later references may establish enduring mountains or architecture, but a later screenshot
cannot prove an early village already had its future city layout.
