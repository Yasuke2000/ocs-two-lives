# Parties

If you want to follow the same story with friends, form an FTB Teams party before making your Oath. Your party shares quest-book progress. Each soul keeps its own reputation, skills and boss eligibility.

1. The leader runs `/ftbteams party create Dawn`. Replace `Dawn` with the party name you want.
2. The leader invites a player with `/ftbteams party invite Alex`.
3. Alex runs `/ftbteams party join Sam` if **Sam is the leader**. Use the leader's player name here.
4. Each player can use `/ttl party` to check the leader, roster, Oath and boss-credit information. Use `/ftbteams party leave` to leave.

The current limit is **five members including the leader** and may change. A party can adventure together without paying a nation charter.

## Agree on the Oath

At the Crossroads, the leader chooses whether to stand with Tempest or walk the Outcast road. The choice applies to eligible party members. An offline member receives the pending Oath when they return. An undecided Life 2 soul joining a sworn party takes its path. If you already swore the opposite Oath, the party removes you after the next membership check. If nobody has chosen yet, a sworn joiner can establish the party's path. Discuss it before inviting them.

A later Atonement or Corruption inside the party lets you stay. Leaving and trying to rejoin checks your path again.

## Fight together

Gather before engaging the boss and stay near the fight through its defeat. The server takes a roster during the encounter. A party member sharing a fighter's credit must be on that roster, online, in Life 2, in the same dimension and close enough when the boss falls. Spectators do not qualify. Under the current rules (may change), the sharing distance is twice that boss's encounter range. Where the boss awards Skill Cores (tier 3 and above), at most one extra core per party goes to members who qualify through shared credit alone.
