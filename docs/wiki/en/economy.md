# Coins, shops and the Free Guild

For your first paid job in Life 2, visit a Free Guild receptionist or type `/ttl guild board desk`. Check the work, rank requirement and reward before accepting. If the first listing suits you, use `/ttl guild take 1 desk`. See your progress with `/ttl guild status` and collect the reward with `/ttl guild turnin desk`. Kills and visits are tracked after acceptance. Bring delivery items in your inventory for hand-in. You can drop a job with `/ttl guild abandon <id>`.

## Contracts from Fuze

Receptionists offer the ordinary board. Fuze runs the high-rank contracts at the Blumund guildhall. The desk currently rolls three daily and two weekly jobs. Board sizes and rank thresholds may change. Fuze has two additional weekly contracts starting at rank B (140 lifetime bounty points), and you can hold three active jobs in total. The daily board rolls on the UTC date and the weekly board on Monday UTC. Quest-book progress, personal renown and guild rank have separate uses.

Use `/ttl guild board fuze` and `/ttl guild take 1 fuze` to read and accept the first of Fuze's listings. Most completed Fuze contracts can go to a receptionist. Fuze wants the reports from **Magisteel for the Dwarven Crown** and **Ghosts of the Ogre Village** personally because their findings matter to the story. Speak to Fuze or use `/ttl guild turnin fuze` for those. Fuze accepts his own contracts. Return ordinary jobs to the desk.

## Bank and trade

The currency is Tensura's physical coins. The current exchange rate (may change) is **10 bronze = 1 silver, 10 silver = 1 gold, 10 gold = 1 stellar gold**. A wallet holds coins you carry. A **Mana Ledger** lets you exchange denominations, deposit or withdraw from a Guild Coffer you can access, and transfer money to another player's or team's coffer. Search the recipe viewer for “Mana Ledger” to see the six magic ore shards, two amethyst shards and one low-quality magic crystal used to craft it. Banking and network trading are available outside the Mortal realms in Life 2.

Use a Lightman's Currency trader to run a player shop. Set the goods, price and stock in its trading interface. A **Merchant's Scrying Glass** opens the network traders. Auction House sales currently incur a **5% fee** (may change). Guild Coffers currently earn no interest.

Keep enough coins in your inventory or equipped wallet for pack fees. Charters, upkeep and public duel damage already use this economy. The planned altar fee of five silver and identification fee of two gold are configured but the current player actions do not collect them. These prices may change. A nation has its own treasury for claimed land. To put ten silver there, use `/ttl nation deposit silver 10`. Depositing in your personal Guild Coffer will not fund nation upkeep.
