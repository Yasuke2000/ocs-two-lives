# Your two lives

You wake up at **Wayfarer’s Rest** as a human. Your first death ends this life, even if another player deals the last blow. There is time to settle in. Read *A Wayfarer’s Primer*, open the quest book and start with **Only Once**. A bed, food and a shield are good reasons to stay close to home for the first night.

## Life 1 in the Mortal Realm

The Mortal Realm has its own Nether and End. Follow the quests that suit the life you want to lead. You can raise animals, work a village, brew potions or make the journey to the Mortal Dragon. The Ledger accepts any five of the nine preceding chapter capstones. The optional finale also asks you to defeat the dragon, survive 30 active Minecraft days and come home. Your first death can send you onward at any point in that story.

## When you die

Your inventory, armour, backpacks and ender chest stay behind. Your soul keeps its XP, advancements, quest progress and karma. Your **Soul Gem** comes with you. In Life 2, right-click it for a brief burst of Regeneration and Resistance. It currently takes 20 minutes to recharge. A Hero or Legend with **Craft** as their strongest axis and at least 150 Craft can also bring back their best carried non-curse enchantment as a book, up to level III. These values may change.

In the **Spirit Corridor**, read your verdict, choose a Last Wish, then decide whether to face its trial before reincarnating. If you close a screen, use `/ttl menu` or speak to the Voice of the World. [Race and skill choices](races-and-uniques.md)

## Your first minutes in Life 2

You arrive near **(1660, −100)** on the northern edge of Jura, on Lerfing’s map of the Tensura continent. The short arrival grace gives you time to get your bearings. Dwargon lies north, Tempest south. Prepare for stronger creatures and denser magicules on the way into the forest. Open **Open Your Eyes** in the quest book to begin the second story. From here, death lets you respawn.

Use `/ttl guide` to reopen the guide, `/ttl status` to check which life you are in, and `/ttl life` to read your first-life record. [How karma works](karma.md) · [Story and Oath](story-and-oath.md)
