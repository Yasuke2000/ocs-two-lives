# The story and your Oath

Start Life 2 with **Open Your Eyes**. Work through **Tempest Rises** until **Crossroads** opens. At Tempest, speak to **Rigurd** or use the Oath button in the quest book. You can reopen the choice with `/ttl story oath`.

**Stand with Tempest** to gain 20 reputation with Tempest and take Rigurd's route. **Walk the Outcast road** to gain 20 with the Outcasts and 15 with the Orc Horde, lose 20 with Tempest and take the title *Marked*. The contact waits on Tempest's south road. A Fallen soul can still stand with Tempest and take the title *Repentant*. These starting values may change.

**Playing with friends?** Talk it over before swearing. The party leader chooses for the party. Members who haven't sworn yet take that path, including eligible members who join later. A newcomer who already swore the opposite Oath cannot join. Walking solo lets you choose your own path.

## Follow the lit quests

The chapter numbers also include side activities. Follow the quest connections to stay on the main story. Completing one lane is enough.

| Act | Where the choice takes you |
| --- | --- |
| I · The Forest Awakens | Help establish Tempest, then take the Oath in chapter 7, **Crossroads**. Follow Rigurd or the Outcast. |
| II · The Armed Nation | In Dwargon, visit the dwarves and meet Gazel. In chapter 8, **The King's Test**, arrange the honour duel on Light. On Dark, copy the smiths' plans or risk regicide. |
| III · The Orc War | After **The Orc Lord**, follow chapter 9, **Two Hundred Thousand**. Defend the lizardmen and feed the surviving orcs, or raid the caverns and lead the Horde. |
| IV · Calamity | In chapter 10, **The Whirlpool**, bring Milim honey and defend Lake Siss, or wake Charybdis for Clayman and face what you unleashed. |
| V · The Holy Knight | Chapter 11, **Mask and Blade**, follows Shizu, Ifrit and the crisis with Falmuth. Choose whether to defend Tempest or march with Falmuth. |
| VI · Walpurgis | In chapter 12, **Walpurgis**, enter Clayman's court. Spar with Carrion and seek Luminous's blessing, or turn against Clayman to take his seat. |
| VII · Harvest Festival | Chapter 13, **Harvest Festival**, brings both roads back to Veldora. The Light finale follows the True Hero story. The Dark finale follows the Demon Lord story. |

For the final awakening quest you need the matching **True Hero** or **True Demon Lord** awakening in Tensura itself. Submit the task after awakening. You can check for an available Legendary separately with `/ttl absorb free`.

When a quest asks you to face a boss, go to the place it names before claiming the fight. If a later quest stays locked, open it and check its prerequisites. A party member's progress can differ from yours.

## Can I change sides?

Once per soul, an eligible player can use `/ttl story flip`. A Dark soul needs to earn **Atonement** through the story hand-ins. A Light soul can open **Corruption** through deeds such as killing Gazel outside the honour duel. Turning changes your path and reputation. Your previous deeds are still part of the story.
