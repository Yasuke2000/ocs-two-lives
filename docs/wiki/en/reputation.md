# Reputation and renown

Use `/ttl rep` after rebirth to see who welcomes you and who might turn you away. Reputation belongs to you and is tracked separately for Tempest, Dwargon, Blumund, Falmuth, the Western Holy Church, the Orc Horde and the Lizardmen of Lake Siss. The Outcast Society appears in your list when its score moves away from zero.

## Read your standing

These are the current bands and **may change**. The names below match the English labels.

| Score | Standing |
| --- | --- |
| −100 to −50 | Hostile |
| −49 to −20 | Unfriendly |
| −19 to 19 | Neutral |
| 20 to 49 | Friendly |
| 50 to 79 | Honored |
| 80 to 100 | Exalted |

At Hostile, faction guards can target you. Dwargon's score feeds Tensura's own dwarf trading and guard system. Hostile is the configured refusal threshold for faction shops, though individual shops must use that rule for it to take effect. Player-owned traders use their shop settings.

## Change your standing

Your starting race and Life 1 deeds can count in different places. For instance, Dwargon values dwarves and craftsmanship. After rebirth, guild contracts show which faction rewards them. Story choices can help one side while alienating another. If you kill a protected faction NPC, you lose standing with its faction. Killing another Life 2 player on a faction's territory can cost local standing too. A wild enemy's species alone does not make it a protected citizen.

For a peaceful route, stand in the Tensura overworld, run `/ttl merit works mark` and place qualifying infrastructure there. Stone bricks, lanterns and oak fences are examples. Check your total with `/ttl merit`. The current award is one point of local reputation per 100 merit where a faction exists, subject to weekly caps (may change).

Most faction scores move one or two points towards Neutral per real week, including elapsed time while offline. Outcast standing currently has no weekly decay. These rates may change.

## What renown measures

Run `/ttl rep renown` for your personal breakdown. Your Life 1 karma tier, boss milestones, faction standings, registered Unique skills and earned bonuses contribute. Renown counts toward the value of a nation you join. Guild rank instead comes from completed bounties, and purchases use coins.
