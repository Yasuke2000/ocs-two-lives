# Frequently asked questions

## How do I join?

Import the official instance in Prism Launcher, launch it and select **OCS Tensura: Two Lives** under Multiplayer. If the server asks you to link Discord, enter the displayed code using `/verify <code>` in the server’s Discord, then reconnect. You can request a code in game with `/discordverify`. If you see a whitelist error, send your exact Minecraft name to Yasuke. [Prism instance download](https://github.com/Yasuke2000/ocs-two-lives/releases/download/prism-instance/OCS-Two-Lives.zip)

## Prism says my mods don’t match. How do I update?

Close Minecraft and launch the imported instance again. Its installer checks for pack updates before Minecraft starts. For a startup crash, open **Edit → Minecraft Log** in Prism and save the error message. [Report a bug](report-bugs.md)

## Can another player end my first life?

Yes. PvP is enabled in Life 1, and a death in the Mortal Realm sends you to the Spirit Corridor. You can check your current life with `/ttl status` and see how it is being judged with `/ttl life`. [Your two lives](two-lives.md) · [Karma](karma.md)

## I closed my choice menu. Where do I get it back?

In the Spirit Corridor, use `/ttl menu` or interact with the glowing Voice of the World in the hall. The menu resumes the choice you still need to make. [Races and Unique Skills](races-and-uniques.md)

## Why do I start outside Tempest after rebirth?

You arrive on the outer rim of Jura, on the road toward Dwargon. Magicules grow stronger as you approach the forest core. The brief protection on arrival gives you time to get your bearings. The rim still has roaming monsters. [The rings of Jura](jura-rings.md)
