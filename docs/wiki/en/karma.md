# Karma and your verdict

Open `/ttl life` during Life 1 to see what the Voice has noticed and what you still need for the next tier. Your verdict uses six scores. Each gives smaller returns as it grows, and your strongest weighted score earns a bonus. If you already spend your days mining, trading with the village on the way home gives a different part of your life a chance to count.

| Axis | What counts in the Mortal Realm |
| --- | --- |
| Valor | Exploring new ground on foot, entering the Mortal Nether or End, slaying different enemies and defeating great foes. |
| Order | Trading with villagers, winning raids and breeding animals. |
| Chaos | Killing villagers, iron golems, wandering traders or players, and starting raids. |
| Craft | Mining diamonds or ancient debris, working an anvil and using a smithing table. |
| Faith | Enchanting, brewing and earning advancements. |
| Longevity | Active play. Moving, looking around and doing things count towards days survived. Standing AFK does little for you. |

Repeated kills of one kind give progressively less credit. Exploration counts distinct areas reached on foot, with a daily cap. Sailing in circles or riding the same road does not keep earning exploration credit.

## What each tier takes

Meet every requirement on a row. An active Minecraft day is 20 minutes of active play. These thresholds may change.

| Verdict | Minimum score | Active days | Great foes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Wanderer | 0 | 0 | — |
| Survivor | 100 | 2 | — |
| Hero | 600 | 7 | — |
| Legend | 1,000 | 30 | At least one |

For Legend, the recorded kill can be the Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden or Elder Guardian. In a group, the killing blow earns the kill statistic.

**Fallen** can accompany any of the four verdicts. Currently it takes at least 60 Chaos and a Chaos score at least as high as Order. A Fallen Hero still receives Hero offers, with a dark race included. You still choose which side to swear to when you reach Tempest.
