# Bosses

For a first tier-1 fight in Life 2, visit the orc camp in the Lake Siss marsh. Gather your party, then enter `/ttl story fight orc_lord`. Geld wakes when someone approaches. The boss bar marks the fight. Stay near the boss through the final blow if you want credit.

## Fixed encounters

Go to the named site in the Tensura overworld and use `/ttl story fight <id>`. The current approach distance is 96 blocks horizontally. If you are too far away, the reply gives the encounter coordinates. Story quests can also lead you there. Either Oath can fight these bosses. A prerequisite in the table applies to the person starting the encounter.

## Rare wild encounters

A Life 2 player travelling through an eligible region gives the server a chance to start an encounter nearby. Watch for the announcement. A wild encounter skips the fixed summoner's progression requirements. Camping at a landmark guarantees no particular spawn time.

- **Jura and Lake Siss.** Watch the water for Charybdis. In Jura, thunderstorms can bring Mimic Echo or Veldora. Shizu and the Supermassive Slime can appear in Jura without a storm.
- **Away from Jura.** Carrion can appear in Eurazania, Clayman in Jistav and the Elemental Colossus in Damargania. Luminous can appear in Lubelius at night.
- **Fixed destinations worth travelling to.** Gazel's wild chance is zero in the current settings. Sylphide, Undine, Akash and the tier-3 Rimuru fight have no wild roll.

The current server limits are one wild boss active at a time, at most two wild starts per Brussels calendar day and at least six hours between starts. Spawn rates, encounter gates and payouts may change.

## Getting credit

Hit the boss, land the final blow, or accumulate the required time in its encounter radius (currently 15 seconds). Be in range when it falls. Nearby eligible teammates can share the tier unlock, kill credit and rewards. Tier 3 and 4 direct contributors earn a Skill Core. Under the current rules, at most one additional eligible teammate per party can receive a shared core. Reputation consequences depend on the opponent.
