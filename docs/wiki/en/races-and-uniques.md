# Choosing a race and a Unique Skill

In the Spirit Corridor you make two decisions. **Your verdict shapes the bodies you can choose. Your Last Wish shapes the skills you can draw.** If you want to play a Slime who protects their friends, choose **Protection**, then choose **Slime** in the reincarnation ceremony.

## Which races can appear?

Slime is always on the list. A higher tier adds races to the possible pool and gives you a longer shortlist. These offer counts and limits may change.

| Verdict | Total offers, including Slime | Races added to the regular pool |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wanderer | 2 | Goblin |
| Survivor | 3 | Human, Elf, Dwarf, Merfolk, Lizardman, Wight, Ghoul |
| Hero | 4 | Ogre, Harpy, Beastfolk, Giant |
| Legend | 5 | Hobgoblin, Dragonewt, High Orc, Vampire, Lesser Daemon |

Hero reserves one offer from its tier and Legend reserves two. A Fallen verdict also reserves an offer from its dark pool. For example, a Fallen Wanderer sees Slime and Orc.

## What does a wish give me?

A wish chooses a pool. The following pairs are examples of the ordinary draw and the optional **Legend echo**.

| Last Wish | Possible draw | Legend echo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Fighter | Wrath |
| Knowledge | Analyst | Great Sage |
| Protection | Guardian | Creator |
| Hunger | Gourmet | Predator |
| Freedom | Traveler | Sloth |
| Creation | Fusionist | Creator |
| Vengeance | Reaper | Wrath |
| Deceit | Falsifier | Envy |
| Dominion | Commander | Pride |
| Survival | Survivor | Greed |

For a **Wanderer or Survivor**, fate ordinarily draws one available skill. Passing your wish's optional Trial of the Soul lets you choose from up to three. A **Hero or Legend** can already choose, even after skipping or failing the trial. A Legend also gains one ordinary bonus skill.

A Legend can choose the wish's echo when the skill still has room. The current caps are five holders per Common skill, three per Rare and three per Legendary, with ten distinct Legendary holders across the server. Gluttony is Rare. If every skill in your pool is taken, rebirth can proceed without a Unique. If another soul takes the final place while you are choosing, select a remaining option.

Later, a willing holder can offer a skill with `/ttl absorb bequest <player> <skill>`. The recipient uses `/ttl absorb accept <giver>`. Awakened True Heroes and True Demon Lords can check eligible Legendaries with `/ttl absorb free`. Each awakening kind can be used once per soul, including across a Reset Counter rebirth.
