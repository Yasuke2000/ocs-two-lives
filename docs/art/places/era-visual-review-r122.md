## Inspected scope

All 58 original images were viewed in five private overview layouts. Twelve were also inspected at original resolution: Tempest, Laura, Kaijin’s smithy, Orc camp, old goblin village, lizardmen caverns, Marris, Clayman’s castle, Englassia, Coleus, Dwargon throne and Sealed Cave. The remaining 46 have overview screening only; small symbols, occupants or implied events could have been missed. The CSV records the exact scope for each file.

The focused views show a small construction-stage village, intact old Laura and an empty former workshop rather than a later metropolis, destroyed/reconstructed Laura or an identifiable duplicated Kaijin. Other focused views show no identifiable later visit/raid/coronation or completed Orc War outcome. These are limited visual observations. Anonymous figures, plain decorative cloth and interpreted architecture do not certify an initial roster, canonical flag or entire old-city plan.

No definite late-state trigger requiring a bitmap revision was found in the viewed scope. Retain the current originals for further source/design review. This is not all 58 canonical-era acceptance, nor a reason to repaint suitable images merely to create another round. Orc troop/species models and counts, actual old-village occupancy, the former shop’s condition, early court/garrison rosters and precise palace/cavern architecture remain unverified.
