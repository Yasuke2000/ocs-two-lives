## Correction to the cave-state brief

The [official episode2 synopsis](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no2)
explicitly states that Rimuru takes Veldora into his body to resolve imprisonment
and then leaves the cave (`OFF-CAV-047`). That supports removing the occupied dragon
prison scene from the selected post-Kijin setting. It does **not** establish that the
sealing skill was already destroyed,or that chains,hexagrams,standing stones and
glass armillary rings physically broke in the cave. The old phrase **seal is spent**
was therefore too strong and is replaced with neutral absence/absorption wording.

The legacy lore already calls its chamber,altar and chains original interpretation;
the place brief now makes that equally clear. No broken-prison rubble,developed
hipokute farm or reinstated Veldora is newly certified for the initial world. The
existing blueprint/art are not regenerated or declared fixed by this documentation.
