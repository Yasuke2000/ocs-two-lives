It supports the C18 era distinction, not a paved highway design: surface material, alignment and construction date are not supplied. This passage cannot establish that such patrol organization already existed immediately after the Kijin naming. Initial settlements and residents remain at the owner's anime S1E10 snapshot.

Volume4 p1–2 places Hinata in her assigned room inside a palace in Lubelius. It does not position that palace relative to the capital, cathedral or summit shrine. Volume8's opening Granbell/Damrada discussion concerns later trade and political control; it does not locate Siltrosso's borders. Volume9 p244 and269 identify Ingrassia's royal capital as an escort destination and a tournament host. They do not establish a lakeside capital or a permanent arena footprint. None of these passages resolves C06, C17 or the smaller western country reservations.
