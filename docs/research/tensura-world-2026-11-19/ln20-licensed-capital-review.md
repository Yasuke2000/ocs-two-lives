# LN20: licensed trial access and a later capital distinction

Reviewed 23 November 2026. This is selected-passage research, not a reading of the complete sample, chapter or volume.

The [ordinary ebookjapan listing](https://ebookjapan.yahoo.co.jp/books/247818/A003344114/) identifies the Japanese GC Novels light novel, distinct from manga volume 20. After closing the normal coupon popup, its trial button opens the [licensed reader](https://ebookjapan.yahoo.co.jp/viewer/trial/B00183344114/). No login, purchase or access restriction bypass was used. Copyrighted screenshots and HTML remain in the private research directory; this repository contains paraphrases and links only.

## What was directly verified

The reader displays a 93-page sample counter. Its contents menu offers the cover, contents, prologue and first chapter. Selected front matter and prologue spreads were inspected, followed by the first chapter heading at reader position 34/93 and its opening at 36/93. These are reflow-viewer positions, not printed Japanese page numbers. Several earlier captures show a loading spinner and are excluded as evidence. Empty extracted body text did not mean that the rendered Japanese pages were unreadable.

**LN20-CAPITAL-001, canon-explicit / LN:** the opening setting sentence of chapter 1 places the scene in former Eurazania, at the planned construction site of Milim's new capital. This supports distinguishing the later capital project from Carrion's earlier capital and its initial buildings. It does not locate either capital numerically, name that earlier capital, identify the current `laura` reservation with the new site, describe a completed city or resolve the guidebook's Rondo naming discrepancy.

The map's initial state remains immediately after Kijin naming, anime S1E10. No LN20 battlefield, later ownership change or new-capital construction is activated. The existing neutral atlas label for Carrion's capital remains appropriate within its source limits; no terrain movement follows from this later-era passage.
