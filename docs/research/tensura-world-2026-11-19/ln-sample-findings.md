# Publisher-hosted LN evidence

The original Japanese public fragments for volumes 2–10 are legally published by GC Novels.
Relevant passages from volumes 2, 3, 5, 6 and 10 were directly read. They add twelve narrowly scoped
`LN*` claims to the ledger; they do not verify complete volumes, English page numbers or absent bearings.
The other fragments were screened, not claimed fully researched. Old-format volume 11/12 fragment URLs
returned 404; this does not imply no licensed sample exists elsewhere.

| Passage | What it supports | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LN2 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime2.htm) | Orc homeland is depleted, at a junction of three demon-lord territories and forest. | No compass bearings or ruler names in that passage. |
| [LN3 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime3.htm) | Frey territory touches Jura. | Does not establish an exact Jia coordinate. |
| [LN5 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime5.htm) | Mountain backdrop and town below Carrion castle; later destruction/evacuation. | Pre/post-destruction eras must be distinguished. |
| [LN6 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime6.htm) | Church base and summit sanctuary reached by stone stairs. | No surveyed country boundary or NW bearing from Lune. |
| [LN10 prologue](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime10.htm) | Amrita ruins are distinguished from the surface city. | Exact burial depth, mound outline and eastward game offset remain interpretation. |
| [LN10 historical passage](https://gcnovels.jp/reading/slime10.htm) | Soma is the older destroyed homeland; Amrita was founded later in a distant refuge. | No primary compass bearing or surveyed ruin footprint. The western Soma lead remains wiki-backed. |

Country adjacency from LN3 strengthens the existing Fulbrosia/Jura contact. LN10 resolves the earlier
assumption that an anime castle frame alone describes the whole Amrita site; it does not justify a
specific mound height. Neither finding gives permission to invent precise sovereign boundaries.

The [8.5 guidebook publisher listing](https://gcnovels.jp/book/47) identifies an official world map and
country profiles. The listing alone is not the map content. No copyrighted text or image is stored here.
