# Coleus — official source and authored placement review

The official [anime outline](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/coleus/outline), STORY / OUTLINE request paragraph, explicitly places the **country** between Ingrassia and Lubelius. The [9 May 2023 production announcement](https://www.ten-sura.com/news/anime/coleus/news/post/8574), STORY and INEI panorama sections, repeats that relationship and publishes a production concept of its desert setting. Both pages were directly read on 20 November 2026. OFF-COL-001 and OFF-COL-002 record their different evidentiary roles.

The [production concept](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/bf4a714bd04266b501beea54ae574464.jpg) was visually inspected: a domed palace stands above the lower town on a rocky hill, with an inclined approach, foreground water and vegetation, and arid surrounding relief. It is a production painting, not an episode frame or a north-oriented survey. Its compass directions, water-body dimensions and elevations cannot be inferred from perspective. The original is cached only outside the repository; this document contains links and paraphrases.

This is a useful authored improvement, **not proof that the canonical capital lies on a straight line between them**: the synopsis describes countries, and neither capital coordinates nor borders are supplied. ## Architecture and era limits

The existing [Coleus blueprint](../../../blueprints/coleus.py), including `hill()` and the palace approach, already supplies an original sandstone outcrop and stair beneath the palace. A second terrain hill would duplicate that work. The map retains its radius-73 clearing, oasis and desert setting; the blueprint and placement data are unchanged. File presence and a source-inspired design are not proof of correct in-game placement or whole-city completeness.

The official outline describes Rimuru's later teaching period. Its succession investigation, roster and palace condition are not automatically the state immediately after the Kijin naming. The broad geographical relationship is used here; the existing era assessment continues to require verification of buildings, residents and rulers. No later OVA event is applied to the initial world.
