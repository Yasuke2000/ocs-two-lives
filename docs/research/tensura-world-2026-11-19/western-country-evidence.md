Reviewed 22 November 2026. This corrects source attribution in the current regional guide and the two older lore entry points. It does not relocate countries or establish surveyed borders.

## What was actually verified

The text of [Taiqi_'s community comparison](https://www.reddit.com/r/TenseiSlime/comments/1suz7yb/new_countries/) reports Morgdahl, Nostraille, Yuzalgh and Yggdonia in west-to-east order on a later Council map. This is the existing **FAN-WES-034** ledger claim, classified `fan-wiki`. The post caption is not independent authentication of the anime frame. A direct public-page/JSON request returned HTTP 403; the indexed text was readable, but no original map image or timestamp was verified through it. Do not promote this to `canon-explicit`.

The [official episode page](https://www.ten-sura.com/anime/tensura/story/no81) displays episode **76**, despite route `no81`. Its synopsis supplies later Council context. All five public selected stills were inspected privately: character close-up, dungeon display, adventuring party, an interior group and a slime display. None exposes the alleged country-label map. This finite selection does not prove the map absent from the episode. Neither the route number nor a fan post date supplies a verified playback timestamp.

The [official introductory world image](https://www.ten-sura.com/4GfGdAp7/wp-content/themes/tensura_portal/assets/images/portal-map/world/intro/map-base.webp) is retained as broad continental reference. It must not be cited as verification of these four names, their ordering or exact borders. The linked Blumund and Jura landscape images remain explicitly cross-region visual analogies.
