**Patch notes 18/11/2026 - EN 1/3: starting out and the world**
The first patch notes! A big update: Life 2 now has a real world to explore. What changed:

**Safer starts**
- New players no longer die at spawn, and the arrival areas are free of hostile mobs.
- Wayfarer's Rest, the Life 1 start, has been refined.

**What you take with you**
- Life 1 items and Tensura items do not carry into Life 2. Only soulbound items and your Soul Gem make the trip.
- Karma now counts active play: time spent actually playing is what shapes your soul.

**A new Tensura world**
- The Tensura world was regenerated with the rings of the Jura Great Forest. Life 2 begins on the safe rim, near the road to Dwargon. Deeper in it gets dark, and the core around Tempest is deadly with magicules. Weak souls beware!
- After rebirth you get 20 seconds of arrival grace to find your feet.

**17+ places to discover**
Sealed Cave, the Spirit gate (leads to the Dwelling of the Spirits), Treyni's shrine, Dragon's Nest, Blumund, Marris, Englassia, Lubelius, Ballachia, Sacred Mountain, Clayman's Castle, City of the Forgotten Dragon, Laura, El Dorado, Frost Palace, Heaven Tower, plus Tempest and Dwargon.

**People to meet**
A whole cast of canon characters now lives in these places. Go and talk to them.

**Patch notes 18/11/2026 - EN 2/3: story, parties, nations and fame**

**The story**
- Swear an Oath to the light or the dark path and follow the story through 7 acts.
- New chapters 7 and 8: Crossroads and The King's Test.

**Parties**
- Team up with up to 5 players. Quests are shared and everyone gets credit for a boss kill.

**Bosses**
- Bosses have fixed lairs and now and then a rare wild sighting. Shizu and Rimuru wait at Tempest.

**Nations**
- A party can become a nation by signing a charter. Claim land (Life 2 only), grow your nation's value and ranks, make alliances or go to war.

**Reputation**
- Every faction has its own opinion of you. Your standing with them also builds your renown.

**Patch notes 18/11/2026 - EN 3/3: economy, Legendary skills, quests and Discord**

**Economy**
- Tensura coins, player shops and the magical Mana Ledger bank.
- The Adventurer's Guild: receptionists hand out work, and Fuze offers high-rank contracts.

**Legendary skills**
- At most 10 souls can hold a Legendary skill at the same time (the Ten Great Demon Lords).
- You can take one over by winning a duel in the Labyrinth, by war, by bequest or through an awakening.

**Quests**
- New quest chapters: from 129 to 155 quests.

**Patch notes 18/11/2026 - NL 1/3: de start en de wereld**

**Patch notes 18/11/2026 - NL 2/3: verhaal, parties, naties en roem**

**Het verhaal**
- Zweer een Oath aan het pad van het licht of van het donker en volg het verhaal door 7 acts.
- Nieuwe hoofdstukken 7 en 8: Crossroads en The King's Test.

**Parties**
- Werk samen met maximaal 5 spelers. Quests worden gedeeld en iedereen krijgt de eer voor een bosskill.

**Bossen**
- Bossen hebben vaste lairs en laten zich af en toe zeldzaam in het wild zien. Shizu en Rimuru wachten in Tempest.

**Naties**
- Een party kan een natie worden door een charter te tekenen. Claim land (enkel in Life 2), laat de waarde en de rangen van je natie groeien, sluit allianties of ga in oorlog.

**Reputatie**
- Elke factie heeft een eigen mening over jou. Je aanzien bij hen bouwt ook je renown op.
